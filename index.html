About Paul Manning

Paul Manning is a bestselling author, entrepreneur and former military and police investigator with more than three decades of experience in complex investigations, leadership and public service.

His career has taken him from military policing and frontline law enforcement to long-term undercover operations targeting organized crime, including the Mafia and outlaw motorcycle gangs.

Today, Paul combines his investigative experience with writing, business development and the creation of technology designed to improve accountability, transparency and the integrity of investigative records.

Based in Niagara Falls, Ontario, he continues to write and comment on policing, organized crime, public accountability, technology and contemporary issues.

Books & Writing

Paul is the author of Ten Seventy Eight: An Undercover Officer's Battle Against Organized Crime and Police Corruption, an Amazon bestseller based on his experiences investigating organized crime.

He is also the author of Ethical Policing and continues to develop further nonfiction and fictional works.

Ten Seventy Eight is also being developed as a screen production, with Paul serving as an Executive Producer.
