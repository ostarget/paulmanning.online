Paul Manning

Paul Manning is a bestselling author, optioned screenwriter, entrepreneur and former military and police investigator with more than three decades of experience in complex investigations, leadership and public service. His work has taken him from frontline policing and undercover operations to business ownership and the development of technology designed to improve accountability and investigative integrity.

He is the author of Ten Seventy Eight: An Undercover Officer's Battle Against Organized Crime and Police Corruption, an Amazon bestseller based on his experiences investigating organized crime. His screenwriting work has been optioned for development, and he continues to write while building innovative investigative platforms including OSTarget and SignalTrak. Based in Niagara Falls, Ontario, Paul also writes on policing, leadership, technology and public policy.
